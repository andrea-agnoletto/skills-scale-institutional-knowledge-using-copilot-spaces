# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor / Business Owner

### Role Summary
The Project Sponsor represents the business investment in a project. They set strategic direction, provide or secure resources, and make decisions that require business authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business objectives, expected benefits, and success measures
- Secure sponsorship, funding, and organizational support
- Resolve escalated scope, priority, or resource trade-offs
- Review progress against intended outcomes and authorize major changes

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- Align with the Product Manager on desired customer and business outcomes, priorities, and trade-offs
- Partner with the Project Manager on project status, risks, dependencies, and escalations
- Provide timely decisions to Developers and other delivery roles when a business decision affects implementation

---

## Tech Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Tech Lead or Architect guides the technical direction of the solution and helps the team make sound engineering decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate architecture, technical standards, and design constraints
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and implementation options
- Support estimation and break down complex technical work
- Facilitate technical design and code reviews, and guide engineering quality

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- Collaborate with Developers on design, implementation, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Work with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and explain technical trade-offs that affect user or product outcomes
- Work with the Project Manager to surface technical dependencies, risks, estimates, and schedule impacts

---

## Quality Assurance / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
The Quality Assurance (QA) or Test Engineer helps ensure that the delivered product meets agreed requirements and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and identify appropriate levels of functional, integration, and regression testing
- Design and execute tests, and support automation of repeatable checks
- Record and communicate defects, quality risks, and validation results
- Assess test coverage and contribute evidence for release readiness

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- Partner with Developers to build testability into the solution, maintain automated tests, and diagnose defects
- Work with the Product Manager to clarify acceptance criteria and verify expected user-facing behavior
- Keep the Project Manager informed about quality risks, unresolved defects, and validation progress

---

## UX Designer / User Researcher

### Role Summary
The UX Designer or User Researcher represents user needs in discovery and delivery through research, interaction design, and usability evaluation.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research to understand needs, workflows, and pain points
- Create and validate user flows, interaction designs, and prototypes
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations
- Share research findings and design decisions with the delivery team

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- Partner with the Product Manager to shape problem statements, validate assumptions, and connect user needs to product priorities
- Collaborate with Developers to assess design feasibility and deliver accessible, usable experiences
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on research and design activities, dependencies, and timing

---

## Operations / Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Operations or Release Manager coordinates operational readiness and the transition of changes into production.

### Responsibilities
- Define deployment and release plans, readiness checks, and operational handoffs
- Coordinate release dependencies, approvals, and rollback planning
- Ensure monitoring, support, and incident-response expectations are in place
- Communicate release status and operational risks

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- Work with Developers on deployment automation, observability, reliability, and production support
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, readiness risks, and stakeholder communications
- Align with the Product Manager on release scope and any customer-facing impact

---

## Agile Facilitator / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Where a team uses an agile framework, the Agile Facilitator or Scrum Master supports effective team collaboration and continuous improvement. This role may be combined with another role or omitted when it does not fit the team's delivery approach.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate team-level planning, reviews, retrospectives, and other agreed ceremonies
- Help the team identify and address impediments
- Encourage continuous improvement and consistent use of the team's agreed practices
- Support team visibility into work and delivery flow

### Interaction with Existing Personas
- Work with Developers to facilitate team processes and remove impediments without directing their technical decisions
- Coordinate with the Product Manager on backlog readiness and team planning, while the Product Manager remains accountable for product vision and prioritization
- Complement, not replace, the Project Manager: focus on team-level facilitation and impediments while the Project Manager remains accountable for the broader project plan, risks, dependencies, and stakeholder reporting

---

These personas describe responsibilities, not mandatory separate positions. Teams may combine or adapt roles to their context, while ensuring each responsibility and decision has a clear owner.
